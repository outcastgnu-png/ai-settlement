# Developer trial

The goal from the writeup's phased plan: **5 outside developers try the kit**
and tell us what worked. This folder holds what a tester gets and where their
answers go. The public release (v0.3.0) was approved on 2026-10-22, so the trial
can run.

| File | For |
|---|---|
| [`TRIAL_SCRIPT.md`](TRIAL_SCRIPT.md) | What to send a tester, and what they do (about 45 minutes) |
| [`FEEDBACK_FORM.md`](FEEDBACK_FORM.md) | The questions they answer |
| [`results/`](results/README.md) | One file per tester, plus the running summary |

## Who to ask

Developers who build or run AI agents, or who have shipped something on the XRP
Ledger. Aim for a mix: at least two who would use TypeScript, at least two who
would use Python, and at least one on Windows.

## When the gate is met

Five testers have got a job paid on testnet (step 2 of the script, from the
example or their own code) and returned the form. Record the date in
`results/README.md`.
